Keep evidence defensible

Hash first, log every step, verify at the end.

1

Hash the evidence

Record the hash before you touch anything.

scarhash hash image.dd > SHA256SUMS
2

Open a custody log

Every entry holds the hash of the previous one.

scarcustody init --name CASE-042 --actor "Omar"
3

Verify at the end

Confirm both the files and the log chain.

scarhash verify SHA256SUMS && scarcustody verify --record
Authorized use only. Follow these steps only on systems and data you own or are authorized to test.

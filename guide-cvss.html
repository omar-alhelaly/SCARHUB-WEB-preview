Score a finding with CVSS

Give each finding a score the client can compare.

1

Write the vector

Choose the metrics honestly: attack vector, complexity, privileges, interaction, scope and impact.

CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N
2

Calculate

Get the score and severity.

scarcvss CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N
3

Track and report

Record the finding in your engagement and build the report.

scarengage show ENG-900
Authorized use only. Follow these steps only on systems and data you own or are authorized to test.

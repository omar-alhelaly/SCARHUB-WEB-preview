Security & responsible use

How SCAR tools are meant to be used, and how to report a problem.

Authorized use only. You are responsible for how you use these tools.

Scope

Use SCAR tools only on systems, networks, drones, logs and data you own or are explicitly authorized in writing to test.

No offensive payloads

The toolkit focuses on analysis, defense, tracking and reporting. It does not ship exploits or evasion features.

Your data stays yours

Tools process data locally. Nothing is uploaded and no telemetry is collected.

Responsible disclosure

Found a problem in a SCAR tool or on this site? Contact us privately first and give us a reasonable time to fix it.

Private chats and privacy

Private chats are visible only to the two people in them. Messages may be reviewed where needed for security and abuse prevention. Do not share passwords or payment details in any chat. Payment proofs are private, re-encoded images that only you and staff can open.
